## 1. Executive Summary

This report establishes the definitive texture key registry inventory and pixel art design matrix specifications for **Hangeul Valley**. 

By auditing `game.js`, Explorer 3 identified **177 unique texture keys** generated by `PixelArtRenderer` methods and legacy canvas texture fallbacks across the game. 

To transform the visual quality from flat, single-tone 2-color place-holders into professional-grade pixel art, this specification provides multi-tone (≥3–4 tones per area) shading, 1px dark outlines, menacing breathing/action poses, glowing energy cores, and detailed metallic/alchemical reflections for:
1. **Dungeon Monsters & Bosses** (`dungeon_green_slime`, `dungeon_skeleton_archer`, `dungeon_goblin_warrior`, `dungeon_boss`)
2. **Dungeon Chest & Loot Items** (`loot_chest`, `loot_coin`, `loot_gem`, `loot_potion`, `loot_scroll`)
3. **Arcade Enemies, Player & Projectiles** (`arcade_player_ship`, `alien_scout`, `alien_shooter`, `alien_elite`, `alien_boss`, `laser_player`, `powerup_weapon`, `powerup_shield`, `powerup_nuke`)
4. **Dungeon & Space Tiles** (`tile_dungeon_*`, `nebula_*`, `planet_*`, etc.)

**Zero Key Drop Invariant**: 100% of existing texture keys in `game.js` are inventoried and mapped. No scene, particle emitter, tilemap loader, or animation system will experience broken key references during implementation.
